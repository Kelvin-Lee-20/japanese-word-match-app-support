## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How do I change the number of pictures on the screen?**
A: Go to the Settings page (the gear icon on the main menu). Under "Test Setup," look for "Items per test." You can switch between 4 pictures (2x2 grid) and 9 pictures (3x3 grid).

**Q: Can I turn off the English words under the pictures?**
A: Yes! Go to Settings and look under "Learning Aids." You can toggle "Show English word" on or off to make the game more challenging.

**Q: Can I turn off the sound effects and vibration?**
A: Absolutely. In the Settings page, under "Sound & Feedback," you can toggle "Sound effects" and "Vibration" on or off based on your preference.

**Q: Does this app work offline?**
A: Yes! Japanese Word Match is designed to be fully offline. You do not need an internet connection to play or learn.
